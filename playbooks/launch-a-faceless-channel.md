# Playbook: launch a faceless channel in one session

Six skills in order, each feeding the next. Run the prompts one after another in the same chat so the agent carries the shortlist, the chosen niche and the titles forward. Budget for the whole chain: about 40 to 45 credits.

| Step | Skill | Prompt to paste | You get |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `niche-finder` | "Find me 3 unsaturated faceless niches in English with real AdSense money. Bend the best one into new ideas and tell me which are still open." | three to five new niche ideas with proof and an openness check |
| 2 | `niche-validator` | "Validate idea #2 from the shortlist as a niche. Go or no-go, with the entry strategy." | verdict, channels breaking in, formats that work, entry plan |
| 3 | `title-formulas` | "Give me titles I can publish in that niche, built on formulas that work there." | about eight titles, each with its formula and proof |
| 4 | `thumbnail-concepts` | "What thumbnails work in that niche right now? Concepts for my first video." | three concepts built from viewed thumbnails, images or prompts |
| 5 | `video-ideation` | "Turn the strongest titles into video ideas for a new faceless channel in that niche." | six ideas ranked on demand proof (a new channel has no fit proof yet) |
| 6 | `transcript-intelligence` | "Tear down the structure of the two strongest outliers you found, and give me a script skeleton for idea #1." | hook verbatim, beat sheet, structure template, skeleton |

Take the skeleton to TubeLab Scriptwriter or write the script yourself. Re-run step 3 monthly: formulas decay.

Rules the chain relies on: `classificationQuality: "neutral"` throughout, `"positive"` in step 4; every number comes from a tool response; estimates are bands. If a step says "thin signal", go back one step rather than forcing it.
