# Playbook: launch a faceless channel in one session

Six skills in order, each feeding the next. Run the prompts one after another in the same chat so the agent carries the shortlist, the chosen niche and the titles forward.

| Step | Skill | Prompt to paste | You get |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `niche-finder` | "Find me unsaturated faceless niche ideas in English with real money in them, ranked." | ten ranked niche ideas, each with proof and a way to earn beyond AdSense |
| 2 | `niche-validator` | "Validate idea #2 from the shortlist as a niche. Go or no-go, with the entry strategy." | Market Size, Unsaturated and Profitable scores, verdict, channels breaking in, formats, gaps, bending ideas, entry plan |
| 3 | `title-formulas` | "Give me titles I can publish in that niche, built on formulas that work there." | about eight titles with proof, plus a table of the formulas behind them and their sources |
| 4 | `thumbnail-concepts` | "What thumbnails work in that niche right now? Concepts for my first video." | patterns that work for the new angle and the familiar problem, three concepts that combine them, images or prompts |
| 5 | `video-ideation` | "Turn the strongest titles into video ideas for a new faceless channel in that niche." | about ten ideas, each with a twist and proof, ranked by potential and fit (a new channel has no formats of its own yet, so the other three sources carry it) |
| 6 | `script-breakdown` | "Tear down the structure of the two strongest outliers you found, and give me a script skeleton for idea #1." | hook verbatim, typed beats with timestamps, layout, skeleton |

Take the skeleton to TubeLab Scriptwriter or write the script yourself. Re-run step 3 monthly: formulas decay.

Rules the chain relies on: `classificationQuality: "neutral"` throughout, and `"positive"` in step 4. Every number comes from a tool response. Everything cited is linked with a URL from tool data: videos, channels, moments, brands and products. A sponsor that was only heard links to the video moment. Estimates are bands. If a step says "thin signal", go back one step rather than forcing it.
