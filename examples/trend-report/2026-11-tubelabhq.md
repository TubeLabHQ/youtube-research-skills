## Learnings

**1. Threat framing beats advice framing this month:** four of six winners open on a loss or a test (demonetized, no views, bubble, "testing your channel"). @TubeLabHQ's flops are the advice-framed titles ("Your Retention Graph Is Telling You…" 2,700 views). Reframe the same data as detection: "WARNING: 7 Faceless Niches Getting Demonetized (The Data)".

**2. Narrate the viewer's last upload, with their numbers:** the two biggest English winners (465K and 127K) open by describing the viewer's own Studio screen ("15 views… 31 views", "I might as well quit") before any promise. Open the next video with "You uploaded Tuesday. 40 views. Here is what our index says happened next."

**3. Contradict the consensus, then prove it with the index:** "It is not. Truthfully, it already did." carried an 806-subscriber channel to 46K. The channel's unfair advantage is proof at scale, so the hook can be "Everyone says automation is dying. We checked 12,000 automation channels. Here is what actually happened."
