## 🔍 Winning Patterns
- **Topics:** The winners start over from zero, show exact earnings from one small thing, or earn in little spare time.
- **Titles:**
  - `[Rich Person] Starts Over at [Humble Job] To Prove It's Not Luck`
  - `Watch Me Start & Sell a [Business] in [N] Days`
  - `How I Made $[Exact Amount] off [Small Number] [Products]`
  - `How I Replaced My Salary Using Just My [Small Time Window]`
- **Hooks:** They open with **a deadline** ("In the next 7 days"), **a doubt** ("Was it all just luck?") or **an exact sum** ("$83,500 from two hoodies").
- **Triggers:** Curiosity ([Dubibubi](https://www.youtube.com/@Dubibubiii)'s [10-day AI test](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEKaDesfQK0)), proof ([Maria Wendt](https://www.youtube.com/@MariaWendt)'s [$23M video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aNiDkDlLA3M)), and little effort ([Molly Keyser](https://www.youtube.com/@MollyKeyser)'s ["1hr a day"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GVMpbOGsMIg), [Alex](https://www.youtube.com/@alexpdfbusiness)'s ["laziest way"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DWAxo1Hwvyc)).
- **For your channel:** Keep putting money on the line, like your [$10,000 debate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uzr0gv4N4-s) at 1.68X. Add a deadline, like [Carson Reed](https://www.youtube.com/@carsonreed16) at 26.95X.
