## Learnings

**1. Say the credential as a number inside the first 20 seconds:** four of seven winners open with a count ("1,100 business owners", "120 coaches", "I'm 52", "gone broke twice") before the thesis. The channel's hooks lean on tone and address; put the portfolio figure first. Example title and open: "I Scaled 100+ Companies. They All Fixed the Same 4 Things" starting with the count of companies and the revenue they add up to.

**2. Economics explainers are the format of the month, and faceless channels own it:** Cost Anatomy (9,200 subscribers) took 349,097 views at ratio 35.0 and three smaller channels repeated the template. The channel can run the same template with first-hand numbers instead of public filings. Example: "The Economics of Owning a Gym (I Owned 6)".

**3. Threat beats inspiration this month:** "rude awakening", "the part that should scare you" and "wish I knew" outperformed, while the channel's two softest titles of the sample ("If I could start over in business, I'd do this sooner", ratio 0.36; the Dolly Parton personal-brand video, 0.38) were its flops. Keep the second-person address, change the frame from invitation to warning. Example: "STOP Starting Businesses. Buy One. (Here's How)".
