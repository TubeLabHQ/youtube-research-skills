# 🔥 Channel Roast: [Alex Hormozi](https://www.youtube.com/@AlexHormozi)

Run: 2026-11-19 · Live TubeLab MCP · `skills/roast-channel/SKILL.md`.

- **Diagnosis:** The channel is growing. Its last six settled uploads have a median of about 852K views, 2.5 times the 340,500 median of its latest 120.
- **Positioning:** It teaches business owners and ambitious young people to grow a business and get rich. Every description sells a free scaling roadmap from Acquisition.com.
- **Assets:** He runs a portfolio of 10 companies and has seen 10,000 businesses. Five of the last six settled uploads open on that.

## ✅ What Works

## ⚠️ What Holds It Back
1. **The "for a Stranger" titles get a fifth of the views the same format gets when titled as his plan.**
   - [Building a $3,000,000 Business for a Stranger in 31 Minutes | Scale or Fail](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j2TZMFkj71Q) · 289,671 views, 0.56x, 2026-07-10
   - **Fix:** Title every makeover the way the roofing episode was: "How I Would Build a $[Amount] [Business Type]".
2. **This title promises a business restart, and the video is a lecture on company culture.**
   - [If I could start over in business, I’d do this sooner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8QfAfCbOXs) · 186,032 views, 0.36x, 2026-10-16
   - **Fix:** Title it by what it teaches, and keep "start over" for videos that lay out a restart plan.
3. **Group coaching episodes are the weakest series on the channel.**
   - [Helping 6 Business Owners Scale in 33 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0EqJD2o-Mnk) · 98,984 views, 0.19x, 2026-04-24
   - **Fix:** Give each episode one named business, a deadline and what it could lose, as the theme park episode did.
