## 5. Three directions for your next thumbnail

1. **Proof object for "How I Would Build a $10M Service Business"-type videos:** presenter on the right, a single revenue chart or a printed P&L on the left with the headline number legible at 320 pixels, text of 3 words that is not in the title ("boring, but $10M"), warm background to break the channel's dark palette, arrow from the text to the number.
2. **Cast and stakes for the competition series:** keep the eight contestants and the presenter, add the stake as text ("$100,000 ON THE LINE", 4 words), split layout with the presenter left and the cast right, high contrast; the title already carries the format, so the image should carry the money.
3. **Question-command for the "ambitious and in your 20s" line:** one face, 4 words that ask rather than state ("why are you still broke?"), high contrast on a warm background, no object; borrows the archetype of #6 and #8 while staying inside the channel's authority.

## 6. Do / don't

Do: put the result in the image when the title withholds it (4 of the 12 winners show a book, chart, worker or calendar) · keep text at 3 to 4 words and make it say something the title does not (9 of 12 winners) · use an arrow when there is an object to point at (3 of 12).
Don't: ship low-contrast webcam frames (the channel's 0.36 video is the lowest-contrast image in the set) · repeat the title in the image (the only echoes on the board are a suspected placement and a diagram) · copy row 1's layout on the strength of its ratio; nothing flagged red_flag, but the engagement shape marks it as paid.
