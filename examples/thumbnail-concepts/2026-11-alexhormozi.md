# 🖼️ Thumbnail Concepts: "Change My Mind, Win $10,000"
Run: 2026-11-09 · Credits used: 4 · Live TubeLab MCP · `skills/thumbnail-concepts/SKILL.md`.

- **Lead with:** Concept 1, because the same one-against-a-room format reached 5.06x with the hot take quoted.
- **Keep from the channel:** Alex's face and beard, the flannel or black tee, and bold white capitals with one coloured word.

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/Uzr0gv4N4-s/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uzr0gv4N4-s)

- **Unique angle:** Alex debates a room of entrepreneurs on his hardest beliefs, and the best counterargument wins $10,000 cash.
- **Familiar problem:** Whether building a business is worth giving up weekends, balance and family time.

### 1. The Quoted Hot Take

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/7XUpSDsiFHM/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7XUpSDsiFHM)

- **Combines:** Hot take in quote marks + one red word.
- **Based on:** [CLASH](https://www.youtube.com/@Clash) · [NFL MVP vs 10 Die-Hard Football Fans (Ft. Cam Newton)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7XUpSDsiFHM) · 1,684,093 views, 5.06x
- **Image:** Alex fills the lower centre mid-argument, with a semicircle of seated entrepreneurs behind him. The eye goes from the quote to his face.
- **Text:** “NO WEEKENDS” with "WEEKENDS" in red.
- **Prompt:** YouTube thumbnail, 16:9. Bright white studio with ten business owners on director's chairs in a semicircle, slightly blurred. Leave the lower centre empty for a real cut-out photo of the host, mid-sentence and pointing at the camera. Across the top, bold white capitals with a thick black outline, in quote marks: “NO WEEKENDS”, with "WEEKENDS" in red. Sharp contrast, no other text.

### 2. Balance Versus All In

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/LfDhieTY2Aw/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LfDhieTY2Aw)

- **Combines:** Two camps, two labels + a "less than" sign between two values.
- **Based on:** [Leevi Vähäkylä](https://www.youtube.com/@leevi.builds) · [I spent 8 years sacrificing everything (only to realise this)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LfDhieTY2Aw) · 111,679 views, 14.36x
- **Image:** Two white cards sit above Alex, one showing a family and one showing an office. The "<" sign is the hook.
- **Text:** BALANCE < ALL IN
- **Prompt:** YouTube thumbnail, 16:9. Modern room, softly blurred. Two white square photo cards near the top: the left card shows a parent laughing with a child, labelled "BALANCE" in black capitals; the right card shows an empty office lit at night, labelled "ALL IN". A large black "<" sits between them. Leave the bottom centre for a real photo of the host looking straight at the camera.

### 3. Overrated, Across The Table

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/3OBaKb9YOAQ/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3OBaKb9YOAQ)

- **Combines:** The room behind the host + one red word, hands clasped.
- **Based on:** [Nevaeh Souza](https://www.youtube.com/@NevaehSouza) · [I Went to Hollywood for a 1 vs 20 Debate….Something Crazy Happened](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3OBaKb9YOAQ) · 300,438 views, 2.89x
- **Image:** Alex sits at a dark table by a stack of cash, with the panel blurred behind. The eye lands on the cash.
- **Text:** PROFIT IS OVERRATED
- **Prompt:** YouTube thumbnail, 16:9. Dim studio with a warm lamp. A wooden table in front holds a neat stack of $100 bills. Behind it, a seated panel of entrepreneurs sits small and out of focus under studio lights. Leave the right half for a real photo of the host, hands clasped, unsmiling. On the left, stacked white capitals: "PROFIT IS OVERRATED", with "OVERRATED" in red.
