Anchor reflection: the audience already connects "change my mind" with two people face to face at a table with microphones, with the defender's shrug or folded hands and the challenger's gesturing hand, and with the phrase itself in big type. The emotion is tension with a grin underneath, not anger. Symbols: the mic, the table, the raised eyebrow, the arrow between two people.
Competition: the videos on the same idea either print the phrase huge (Larry Forman) or hold up a sign with the number (Hikaru). None shows the money at the point of exchange, none shows the expert's own shrug, and the current thumbnail for this video shows neither the bet nor the shrug: it is a "1 vs 10" format card. We stand out by making the $10,000 physical and putting it in the viewer's seat, and by keeping the text to the number.
Compositions: worth borrowing are the handwritten sign held by the person who pays, a single green or red money number behind or above the subject, the contestant line-up on white, and one subject sharp against a soft crowd. Stay away from illustrated or generated-looking scenes, screaming faces with flying bills, and anything over three words.

## Caveats

- The negative anchor set (`averageViewsRatioTo: 0.8`) returned nothing, so "avoid this" is judged from weak thumbnails inside the positive sets, not from flops.
- Ratios reflect title, thumbnail and topic together; the patterns here are associated with the lift, not proven causes.
- Thumbnails for the current video were viewed as served on 2026-11-05; thumbnails change after publish.
