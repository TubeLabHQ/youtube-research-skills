# 🔬 Reverse Engineering: [MrBeast](https://www.youtube.com/@MrBeast)
Run: 2026-11-12 · Live TubeLab MCP · `skills/reverse-engineer-channel/SKILL.md`.

- **Why it works:** Simple contests with a rule and a cash prize in the title carry the channel, and its "$1 vs" price ladders beat their neighbours in 9 of 12 videos.
- **Sample:** the latest 120 long videos of about 1,000 uploads, from April 2021 to October 2026 · 120 thumbnails viewed · 7 openings and 3 full scripts read
- **Shorts:** 144 Shorts have a median of 233M views, close to 248M for long videos. Shorts titled "[Action], Win $10,000" do best, like [Answer The Call, Win $10,000](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/50G0kIty7Cg) at 1.3B.
- **Start with:** Open your next video with one sentence that gives the setup, the rule and the stakes.

## 🧩 Formats That Work

A video beats its neighbours when it has more views than the median of the videos published around it.

### 1. The "$1 vs" price ladder · 9 of 12 beat their neighbours

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/1WEAJ-DFkHE/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1WEAJ-DFkHE) [![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/KrLj6nc516A/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KrLj6nc516A) [![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/48h57PspBec/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48h57PspBec)

- **Topics:** plane tickets, cars, yachts, hotel rooms, vacations, houses and futuristic tech, then experiences, private islands and dates.
- **Title formula:** "$1 vs $[Big Amount] [Topic]!" · [$1 vs $500,000 Plane Ticket!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1WEAJ-DFkHE) · 613M views, ratio 2.24
- **Thumbnail formula:** [Jimmy smiling, centre] + [cheap version, left] + [luxury version, right] + ["$1" and the big price in white, top corners] + [grey left, bright blue and gold right]
- **Length:** 16 minutes in the middle, from 10 to 21.

## 🚫 What Doesn't Work

### 1. Charity builds · 3 of 13 beat their neighbours

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/5OLs1GWB4OA/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OLs1GWB4OA)

- **What it is:** "I [Built or Helped] [N] [Things or People]" · Jimmy smiling in front of the place built or the people helped, often with BEFORE and AFTER
- **Proof:** [I Built 10 Schools Around The World](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OLs1GWB4OA) · 87M views, ratio 0.32, February 21, 2026
- **How it differs:** It has no contest, no rule and no prize to win, and it runs 10 minutes in the middle.

### 2. Famous-cast battles · 1 of 5 beat their neighbours

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/DXVHmGoCTco/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DXVHmGoCTco)

- **What it is:** "[N] [YouTubers, Streamers or Celebrities] Fight For $1,000,000" · a crowd of famous faces pressed against glass
- **Proof:** [50 Streamers Fight for $1,000,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DXVHmGoCTco) · 133M views, ratio 0.49, April 4, 2026
- **How it differs:** Only the first, in July 2024, beat its neighbours. The title sells the cast, not a place or a rule anyone could play.

### 3. Question titles · 1 of 5 beat their neighbours

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/zo7i8VTpfNM/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zo7i8VTpfNM)

- **What it is:** "Would You [Risk Something] For $[Amount]?" or "Can I [Do Something]?" · Jimmy mid-stunt next to stacks of cash
- **Proof:** [How Much Would You Risk For $500,000?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zo7i8VTpfNM) · 163M views, ratio 0.60, September 27, 2025
- **How it differs:** The titles that work give an order with a prize, like "Survive…, Win…". These ask the viewer a question instead.

## 🎣 Hook Formulas

### 1. Setup, rule, stakes, twist · 3 of 5 strong openings

- **Structure:** setup → rule → stakes → twist
- **Runs:** the first 10 seconds
- **Weak videos:** [50 Streamers Fight for $1,000,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DXVHmGoCTco) opens the same way, so the hook alone does not carry a video.

<details>
<summary>Example: Last To Leave Circle Wins $500,000</summary>

[Last To Leave Circle Wins $500,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zxYjTTXc-J8) · 566M views, ratio 2.07

- **Setup:** "I put 100 people inside of a giant circle"
- **Rule:** "and whoever leaves the circle last"
- **Stakes:** "wins $500,000."
- **Twist:** "But if you touch the red line it's game over"

</details>

### 2. Payoff, preview, first step · 2 of 5 strong openings

- **Structure:** payoff → preview → first step
- **Runs:** the first 15 seconds
- **Weak videos:** Neither weak opening read uses it.

<details>
<summary>Example: Beat Ronaldo, Win $1,000,000</summary>

[Beat Ronaldo, Win $1,000,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0BjlBnfHcHM) · 414M views, ratio 1.51

- **Payoff:** "If this subscriber can beat Ronaldo in a 1V1, I'm going to give him a million dollars."
- **Preview:** "the strongest and the fastest athletes on the entire planet compete against normal people."
- **First step:** "Starting with Tom Brady."

</details>

## 🎬 Script Structure

- **Layout:** Hook 0:00 → setup by 1:00 → re-hook by 1:45 → stakes increase every 1 to 3 minutes → twist near 10:00 → payoff in the final minute
- **What repeats:** A score that climbs on screen, such as the ticket price, the people left or the money banked, plus a new offer every few minutes.
- **How it ends:** The biggest payoff lands in the final minute, and then Jimmy gives viewers a reason to subscribe.

## 🧠 Learnings

1. **Open with the setup, the rule and the stakes, in one sentence.**
   - **Proof:** [Last To Leave Circle Wins $500,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zxYjTTXc-J8) · 566M views, ratio 2.07, August 31, 2021
   - **Run next:** `script-breakdown` · "Write a 10-second opening for my video about [idea], in this order: setup, rule, stakes, then a twist."
2. **Build a "$1 vs" price ladder in your own topic.**
   - **Proof:** [$1 vs $500,000 Plane Ticket!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1WEAJ-DFkHE) · 613M views, ratio 2.24, April 1, 2023
   - **Run next:** `title-formulas` · "Give me 10 titles in the pattern '$1 vs $[Big Amount] [Topic]!' for a channel about [my topic]."
3. **Put a counter on the thumbnail, such as the day or the price.**
   - **Proof:** [Survive 100 Days In Nuclear Bunker, Win $500,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UPrkC1LdlLY) · 371M views, ratio 1.36, August 3, 2024
   - **Run next:** `thumbnail-concepts` · "Thumbnail concepts for '[my title]': my face close up, the place behind me, and a big 'DAY [N]' counter in the top right."
4. **Raise the stakes every few minutes with a bigger round or a new offer.**
   - **Proof:** [$10,000 Every Day You Survive In The Wilderness](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U_LlX4t0A9I) · 416M views, ratio 1.52, June 1, 2024
   - **Run next:** `script-breakdown` · "Outline my [challenge] video as rounds that get bigger every 2 minutes, with a running total on screen and one offer to quit in each round."
5. **Place your own product inside the sponsor segment.**
   - **Proof:** [$10,000 Every Day You Survive In The Wilderness](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U_LlX4t0A9I&t=965s) · 416M views, ratio 1.52, June 1, 2024
   - **Run next:** `monetization-research` · "What product could my channel about [topic] sell, and how could I show it inside sponsor segments the way MrBeast shows Feastables?"

- ❌ **Do not copy the star cast:** [Beat Ronaldo, Win $1,000,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0BjlBnfHcHM) needed Ronaldo and Tom Brady, and even here famous-cast battles beat their neighbours only 1 time in 5.
- ❌ **Do not copy the prize sizes:** [Eat Everything In A Grocery Store, Win $1,000,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plN7JMbadRg) needed a whole grocery store and a $1,000,000 prize. The rule carries over to a small channel, but the amount does not.
- ❌ **Do not copy the charity builds:** [I Built A City To Save Kids From Illegal Labor](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v9QtM6qnG50) thanks several foundations in its description, and charity builds beat their neighbours only 3 times in 13.
