## 🧩 Formats That Work

A video beats its neighbours when it has more views than the median of the videos published around it.

### 1. The "$1 vs" price ladder · 11 of 12 beat their neighbours

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/1WEAJ-DFkHE/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1WEAJ-DFkHE) [![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/KrLj6nc516A/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KrLj6nc516A) [![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/48h57PspBec/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48h57PspBec)

- **Topics:** plane tickets, cars, yachts, hotel rooms, vacations, houses and jobs, then private islands, experiences, a nuclear bunker, dates and futuristic tech.
- **Title formula:** "$1 vs $[Big Amount] [Topic]!" · [$1 vs $500,000 Plane Ticket!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1WEAJ-DFkHE) · 617M views, ratio 2.25
- **Thumbnail formula:** [Jimmy smiling, centre] + [cheap version, left] + [luxury version, right] + ["$1" and the big price in white, top corners]
- **Length:** 15 minutes in the middle, from 10 to 20.
- **Who can copy it:** anyone can copy · the ladder works at any price range, such as $1 vs $1,000 coffee.

## 🚫 What Doesn't Work

### 1. Charity builds · 2 of 13 beat their neighbours

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/5OLs1GWB4OA/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OLs1GWB4OA)

- **What it is:** "I [Built or Helped] [N] [Things or People]" · Jimmy smiling in front of what was built, often with BEFORE and AFTER
- **Proof:** [I Built 10 Schools Around The World](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OLs1GWB4OA) · 87M views, ratio 0.32, February 21, 2026
- **How it differs:** It has no contest, no rule and no prize to win, and it runs 10 minutes in the middle.

### 2. Famous-cast battles · 1 of 5 beat their neighbours

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/DXVHmGoCTco/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DXVHmGoCTco)

- **What it is:** "[N] [YouTubers, Streamers or Celebrities] Fight For $1,000,000" · a crowd of famous faces pressed against glass
- **Proof:** [50 Streamers Fight for $1,000,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DXVHmGoCTco) · 135M views, ratio 0.49, April 4, 2026
- **How it differs:** Only the July 2024 one beat its neighbours. The title sells the cast, not a place or a rule anyone could play.

### 3. Question titles · 1 of 5 beat their neighbours

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/zo7i8VTpfNM/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zo7i8VTpfNM)

- **What it is:** "Would You [Risk Something] For $[Amount]?" or "Can I [Do Something]?" · Jimmy mid-stunt next to stacks of cash
- **Proof:** [How Much Would You Risk For $500,000?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zo7i8VTpfNM) · 164M views, ratio 0.60, September 27, 2025
- **How it differs:** The titles that work give an order with a prize, like "Survive…, Win…". These ask the viewer a question instead.

## 🎣 Hook Formulas

### 1. Payoff, preview, first step · 3 of 5 strong openings

- **Structure:** payoff → preview → first step
- **Runs:** the first 20 seconds
- **Weak videos:** [I Built 10 Schools Around The World](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OLs1GWB4OA) opens close to this, so the order alone does not carry a video.
- **Who can copy it:** anyone can copy · it needs a payoff worth naming, not a big budget.

### 2. Setup, rule, stakes · 2 of 5 strong openings

- **Structure:** setup → rule → stakes → twist or first step
- **Runs:** the first 10 seconds
- **Weak videos:** [50 Streamers Fight for $1,000,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DXVHmGoCTco) opens the same way, so the hook alone does not carry a video.
- **Who can copy it:** anyone can copy · it is one sentence, and any challenge has a setup, a rule and stakes.

## 🎬 Script Structure

- **Layout:** Hook 0:00 → first step by 0:20 → re-hook by 1:10 → stakes increase every 1 to 3 minutes → twist two thirds in → payoff in the final minute
- **What repeats:** A number that changes on screen, such as the ticket price, the circle size or the people left, plus offers to quit for cash.
- **How it ends:** The biggest tier or the winner lands last, and then Jimmy asks viewers to subscribe.
- **Who can copy it:** anyone can copy · the beats are free; only the size of each tier costs money.

## 🧠 Learnings

1. **Open with the setup, the rule and the stakes, in one sentence.**
   - **Proof:** [Last To Leave Circle Wins $500,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zxYjTTXc-J8) · 567M views, ratio 2.07, August 31, 2021
   - **Who can copy it:** anyone can copy · it costs one sentence.
   - **Run next:** `script-breakdown` · "Write a 10-second opening for my video about [idea], in this order: setup, rule, stakes, then a twist."
2. **Build a "$1 vs" price ladder in your own topic.**
   - **Proof:** [$1 vs $500,000 Plane Ticket!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1WEAJ-DFkHE) · 617M views, ratio 2.25, April 1, 2023
   - **Who can copy it:** anyone can copy · scale the top price to what you can film.
   - **Run next:** `title-formulas` · "Give me 10 titles in the pattern '$1 vs $[Big Amount] [Topic]!' for a channel about [my topic]."
3. **Put a counter on the thumbnail, such as the day or the price.**
   - **Proof:** [Survive 100 Days In Nuclear Bunker, Win $500,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UPrkC1LdlLY) · 372M views, ratio 1.36, August 3, 2024
   - **Who can copy it:** anyone can copy · it is text on a picture.
   - **Run next:** `thumbnail-concepts` · "Thumbnail concepts for '[my title]': my face close up, the place behind me, and a big 'DAY [N]' counter in the top right."
4. **Raise the stakes every one to three minutes with a bigger tier or a new offer.**
   - **Proof:** [$1 vs $500,000 Plane Ticket!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1WEAJ-DFkHE&t=83s) · 617M views, ratio 2.25, April 1, 2023
   - **Who can copy it:** anyone can copy · the tiers can be cheap as long as each one is bigger.
   - **Run next:** `script-breakdown` · "Outline my [challenge] video as rounds that get bigger every 2 minutes, with a running total on screen and one offer to quit in each round."
5. **Place your own product inside the video, not only in the description.**
   - **Proof:** [$1 vs $100,000,000 Car!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KrLj6nc516A&t=214s) · 591M views, ratio 2.16, September 16, 2023
   - **Who can copy it:** needs their asset, a product of your own · until you sell one, this step waits.
   - **Run next:** `revenue-maxxing` · "What product could my channel about [topic] sell, and how could I show it inside my videos the way MrBeast shows Feastables?"

- ❌ **Needs their asset: the famous show at full scale** · budget: [$456,000 Squid Game In Real Life!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0e3GPea1Tyg) rebuilt every set for 456 players. Borrow a game's rules, not its sets.
- ❌ **Needs their asset: the star cast** · fame and access: [Beat Ronaldo, Win $1,000,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0BjlBnfHcHM) needed Ronaldo, and even here cast battles beat their neighbours 1 time in 5.
- ❌ **Needs their asset: the prize sizes** · budget: [Eat Everything In A Grocery Store, Win $1,000,000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plN7JMbadRg) needed a whole store and $1,000,000. The rule carries over, the amount does not.
