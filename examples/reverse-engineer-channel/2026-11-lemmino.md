# 🔬 Reverse Engineering: [LEMMiNO](https://www.youtube.com/@LEMMiNO)
Run: 2026-11-11 · Live TubeLab MCP · `skills/reverse-engineer-channel/SKILL.md`.

- **How it grew:** Median views rose from 2.65 million to 8.45 million after three lists a month became one documentary every six months.
- **Sample:** 120 of 171 long videos, the 51 oldest missing, from January 2014 to October 2025 · 120 thumbnails viewed · 7 openings read
- **Shorts:** None posted.
- **Start with:** Pick one named, real, unsolved case, and open the video on its exact date and place.

## 🚀 Turning Points

### 1. Rage Comics stop, and a game hit gets a sequel · October 2014

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/Pqn-OH-jbrs/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pqn-OH-jbrs)

- [Top 10 Facts - Five Nights at Freddy's [Part 1]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pqn-OH-jbrs) · 15M views, 2.91× the channel average, 23 Oct 2014
- **Before:** The 34 videos before it mixed Top 10 Facts with Rage Comics. Their median was 3.05 million views.
- **After:** From it on, 25 of the next 26 videos were Top 10 Facts, including a Part 2. Their median was 4.25 million views.

### 2. Standalone mystery documentaries begin · July 2016

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/mUP0tx7Ib2w/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mUP0tx7Ib2w)

- [The Dark Side of the Web](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mUP0tx7Ib2w) · 8.0M views, 1.55×, 2 Jul 2016
- **Before:** The nine videos before it were mostly long Top 10 Facts lists and a Q&A. Their median was 2.7 million views.
- **After:** It and the next six documentaries had a median of 8.0 million. The 11 lists made alongside them had 2.6 million.

### 3. The lists end, and documentaries are all that is left · May 2018

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/I2O7blSSzpI/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I2O7blSSzpI)

- [Cicada 3301: An Internet Mystery](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I2O7blSSzpI) · 43M views, 8.35×, 19 May 2018
- **Before:** The 19 videos before it mixed lists, documentaries and channel updates. Their median was 3.0 million views.
- **After:** The 19 videos since then hold no lists. Their median is 8.4 million views, and 12 of them beat the average.

## ✅ Moves to Copy

1. **Build each video around one named, real, unsolved case.**
   - **Proof:** [Cicada 3301: An Internet Mystery](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I2O7blSSzpI) · 43M views, 8.35×, 19 May 2018
   - **Run next:** `video-ideation` · "Give me video ideas for my channel where each video covers one named, real, unsolved case, the way LEMMiNO covers Cicada 3301 and Flight 370."
2. **Put the case's name in a plain title, and promise a search, not an answer.**
   - **Proof:** [The Vanishing of Flight 370](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kd2KEHvK-q8) · 30M views, 5.83×, 14 Apr 2019
   - **Run next:** `title-formulas` · "Write titles in the pattern 'The {Vanishing / Search / Legend} of {named case}' for my video about {my case}."
3. **Use one illustrated object on a dark background with a single red accent.**
   - **Proof:** [The Search For D. B. Cooper](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CbUjuwhQPKs) · 24M views, 4.66×, 27 Apr 2020
   - **Run next:** `thumbnail-concepts` · "Thumbnail concepts for my video {my title}: one flat illustrated figure on a dark background, one red accent, no face and no text, like LEMMiNO's D. B. Cooper thumbnail."
4. **Open on the date, the place and the event, then the mystery.**
   - **Proof:** [The Kennedy Assassination: Inside the Book Depository](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u7euN1HTuU) · 15M views, 2.91×, 24 Jul 2023
   - **Run next:** `script-breakdown` · "Break down the first minute of LEMMiNO's The Vanishing of Flight 370 and draft a cold open in the same shape for my video about {my case}."
5. **Grow first with a cheap list series on popular topics, then move to the big format.**
   - **Proof:** [Top 10 Facts - Five Nights at Freddy's [Part 1]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pqn-OH-jbrs) · 15M views, 2.91×, 23 Oct 2014
   - **Run next:** `niche-bending` · "Bend LEMMiNO's 'Top 10 Facts - {topic}' list format into {my market}, and show which topics would carry it."

## ❌ Moves to Skip

1. **One two-hour documentary a year:** It needs an audience that already waits for you. [Shouting at Stars: A History of Interstellar Messages](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uFI5WpK2sgg) runs 2.5 hours and got 4.8M views, 0.93×.
2. **Subscriber milestone Q&As:** None of the five beat the average. [2,000,000 + Q&A + Changing Channel Name?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ywd0SKv3P8E) got 838,000 views, 0.16×.
3. **Joining big charity drives:** These ride a campaign run by other creators, and both did badly here. [Trees #TeamTrees](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GbetzV4l4CY) got 908,000 views, 0.18×.
