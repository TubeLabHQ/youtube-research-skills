## 4. Gaps and bent ideas

- Gap: the specificity gap. Every outlier format in section 3 that came from a channel under 50K subscribers names one business type (SaaS, trading cards, consulting, home services). The newcomers in section 1 that fail are the ones talking about "business" in general.
- Gap: the "[guru]'s advice applied to [my niche]" format is proven on three small channels (Raath 166K views on 7K subs, Kruse 30K on 1.6K, Perzon 26K) and has not been pointed at any trade.
- Bent idea 1 (swap the audience): "Alex Hormozi's Advice for Plumbers / Dentists / Gym Owners", one practitioner per trade with that trade's real numbers. Parents: Raath and Kruse proof lines above.
- Bent idea 2 (keep the format, name the business): "If I Started a [Lawn Care / HVAC / Bookkeeping] Business in 2027, I'd Do This" from someone who runs one. Parents: Henry SaaS and Healthpreneur above; Raath proves it at 70,000 views on a 7K-subscriber channel ("If I Started a Consulting Business in 2026 I'd Do This" · 70,000 · 1.3 · 2026-09-05).
- Bent idea 3 (fuse two formats): the contrarian alarm narrowed to one owner segment, "This is the hardest year ever to run a restaurant (and the 3 that are growing)". Parents: Alex M H Smith and Brandon Bryant above.

## 7. Caveats

- Sample: 20 channels (12 on-topic) and 20 outliers (18 after excluding likely paid placements), one trimester, English. Under 15 on-topic channels is a modest sample; the direction is clear, the medians are rough.
- The seeded-random channel sample is dominated by tiny channels; `avgViewsToSubscribersRatio` above 1 on a 17-subscriber channel means nothing. Ratios were read only on channels above 1,000 subscribers.
- Index versus live: the channel search reported medians of 29,000 for Razvan and 19,000 for Raath; the live `get_channel` reads returned 9,200 and 35,000 on smaller samples (9 and 8 videos). Section 1 uses the index values, the leader paragraph uses the live values.
- Monetization and audience are estimates shown as bands; two channels carry a revenue band with no AdSense flag.
- Not checked: Shorts, other languages, comments, sponsor rates.
