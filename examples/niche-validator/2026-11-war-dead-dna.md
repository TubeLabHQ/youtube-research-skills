## 🎯 Gaps and Ideas
- **Leaders never cover:** The WWII leaders tell battles and the enemy's view, and the cold case leaders tell murders. None of them names a soldier buried as unknown.
- **The cold case title on a soldier:** Cold case channels already use "DNA gave her a name", as in [CANADA 1917 Cold Case — 108 Years Later, DNA Gave the Woman in the Well Her Name](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K9YgtGkc1DE) (4.6×). Use it for one soldier per video.
- **Stay narrow where the rival is broad:** Hooper Britt mixes shipwrecks, the Civil War and Hitler's deputy. Own WWII and Korea alone, and tell the family's side of the wait.

## 🧭 Entry Plan
- **Angle:** One WWII unknown per video, named by DNA, told as a cold case with a place and a year up front.
- **First titles:** "NORMANDY 1944: He Was Buried as Unknown for 80 Years. Then DNA Named Him" · "PEARL HARBOR 1941: His Grave Said Unknown. 80 Years Later, His Niece's DNA Named Him" · "BELGIUM 1945: His Family Searched for 79 Years. The Answer Was in a Grave Marked Unknown"
