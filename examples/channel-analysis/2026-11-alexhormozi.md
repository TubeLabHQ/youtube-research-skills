# 📊 Channel Analysis: [Alex Hormozi](https://www.youtube.com/@AlexHormozi)

Run: 2026-11-09 · Live TubeLab MCP · `skills/channel-analysis/SKILL.md`.

- **Diagnosis:** The channel is growing. Its last six uploads, not counting this week's, got a median of 693K views, double the usual 341K.
- **Positioning:** It teaches ambitious people in their 20s and 30s to get rich and grow a business, backed by his $250M-a-year portfolio.

## ✅ What Works

### 1. His business-makeover series took off when the title put a business at risk

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/xRxVpjeHmeA/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xRxVpjeHmeA)

- [Can I Save This Failing Theme Park in 90 Days?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xRxVpjeHmeA) · 1,834,201 views, 3.67x the channel's average, 2026-09-11
- **Why:** It names a business, sets a deadline and shows a "We're going bankrupt" post, so viewers cannot guess the ending.

### 2. "If I had to start over" videos work when they name a dollar goal

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/o64cI6tebnU/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o64cI6tebnU)

- [How I Would Build a $10M Service Business (If I Had to Start Over)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o64cI6tebnU) · 1,468,276 views, 2.85x average, 2026-08-29
- **Why:** An episode with the same "You have 90 days" thumbnail got 166,832 views, so the title likely made the difference.

### 3. Titles that describe the viewer get his biggest views

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/UDBkiBnMrHs/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UDBkiBnMrHs)

- [If you're ambitious but inconsistent, please watch this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UDBkiBnMrHs) · 2,107,356 views, 4.28x average, 2025-12-12
- **Why:** It names a trait viewers are proud of and a flaw they admit, and it is the top video in the sample.

## ⚠️ What Holds It Back
1. **Makeover episodes titled as lessons lost views each time: 287K, then 235K, then 167K.**
   - [$100M Worth of Marketing Knowledge in 32 Minutes | Scale or Fail](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B-ogfFiQpXg) · 166,832 views, 0.34x average, 2026-08-07
   - **Fix:** Title each Season 2 episode by the business, the deadline and what could go wrong, as the theme park episode did.
2. **The 2027 follow-up swapped a thumbnail about the topic for a selfie that says "stop wasting time."**
   - [My Actual Social Media Strategy For 2027 - The Algorithm](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZTSI3DDP_4A) · 228,670 views, 0.46x average, 2026-08-15
   - **Fix:** Bring back the platform logos and studio portrait from [the 2026 version](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dMZ-n2KSlxE), which got 926,875 views at 1.89x.
3. **This "start over" title names no goal, and its thumbnail is a webcam frame.**
   - [If I could start over in business, I'd do this sooner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8QfAfCbOXs) · 180,103 views, 0.36x average, 2026-10-16
   - **Fix:** Name the goal, as [How I Would Build a Personal Brand in 2027](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5rOIS7GHD5Y) did a week later with 772,439 views at 1.56x.
