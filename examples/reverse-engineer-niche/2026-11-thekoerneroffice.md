# 🧭 Reverse Engineering: competitors of [The Koerner Office](https://www.youtube.com/@thekoerneroffice)
Run: 2026-11-19 · Live TubeLab MCP · `skills/reverse-engineer-niche/SKILL.md`.

- **What wins:** one surprising, cheap business or machine with a money figure on the thumbnail. Nine winners beat their own channel's median by 9x to 420x.
- **Sample:** 11 channels (11 emerging, 0 established) · 11 winners and 10 flops cited · 26 thumbnails viewed · 13 openings read
- **Start with:** a list of machines or garage businesses. Three channels under 50K subscribers win with it, and Koerner has not made one in three months.

Adrian Dimac was left out: only seven long videos in three months, mostly off topic. Side Hustle Nation had no video at twice its median.

## 📺 Working Formats

- **Format:** a narrated breakdown of a business you could own, often faceless, with a money figure on the thumbnail.
- **Length:** mostly 18 to 27 minutes. Koerner's top three run 33 to 35 minutes.
- **Where to differ:** Koerner has what faceless channels lack, a real owner on camera. Keep that, and borrow their object-first thumbnails.

## 🧩 Patterns That Work

### 1. A list of machines or garage businesses you can buy · 3 winners on 3 channels

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/6nPrEp4fgjY/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6nPrEp4fgjY) [![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/-2H5Mne7CVU/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-2H5Mne7CVU) [![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/pTxN59xCd4c/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTxN59xCd4c)

- **Title formula:** "[Number] [Unseen / Tiny / Poor Man's] [Garage Businesses / Machines] That [Cost Less Than $X / Make $X a Week]"
- **Thumbnail:** [one machine, large, centre] + [money figure per day or week] + [garage or workshop behind]
- **Hook:** a place you already own → what it could earn → the list ahead
- **Angle:** a cheap machine in your garage that turns into steady cash.
- **Anchors:** garage, kitchen table, "poor man's", a price under $1,000.
- **New and specific:** a real machine you can picture, plus a cost and a payout.
- **Who can copy it:** anyone. All three are faceless.

### 2. Boring businesses with a monthly figure · 3 winners on 3 channels

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/K9kXTEcf6i4/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K9kXTEcf6i4) [![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/zZohb9W-TiA/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zZohb9W-TiA) [![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/z15zWnsaMI0/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z15zWnsaMI0)

- **Title formula:** "[Number] [\"Boring\" / Low-Tech / Spare Room] Businesses [Making Ordinary Guys / Making People] [$ per Month or Rich] ([Fully Automated / Low Competition])"
- **Thumbnail:** [one plain object or scene, a mailbox or a bed] + [the money figure, large] + [a face or cartoon reacting]
- **Hook:** the life it gives you, or the obvious picks to skip → the list ahead
- **Angle:** dull work that ordinary people quietly get rich from.
- **Anchors:** "boring", "ugly", "ordinary guys", $ per month.
- **New and specific:** the figure is per month or per day, and the person is ordinary.
- **Who can copy it:** anyone. Two of the three are faceless.

- **New and specific:** a familiar place, then a number nobody expects.
- **Who can copy it:** anyone for the explainers. The interview version needs insider trust: owners who will share their numbers.

## 🎯 Gaps To Win

### 1. Lists of machines and garage businesses

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/6nPrEp4fgjY/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6nPrEp4fgjY)

- **Proof:** [John's Vault](https://www.youtube.com/@Johns-Vault) · [13 UNSEEN GARAGE Businesses That Cost Less Than $1000 to Start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6nPrEp4fgjY) · 662,000 views, 98.8x, Aug 2026
- **Why it fits:** Koerner's own list, "11 Proven Businesses My Viewers Started With Almost No Money", got 109,000 views, 1.5x. A list of machines he owns or has seen would add proof the faceless channels cannot.

### 2. "I bought it" tests with a day count

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/LHARCU4J6L8/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LHARCU4J6L8)

- **Proof:** [Brian Beers](https://www.youtube.com/@brianbeers) · [I Bought 2 Vending Machines… The Earnings Were Crazy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LHARCU4J6L8) · 588,000 views, 28x, Aug 2026
- **Why it fits:** Koerner's one test in three months, [I Tried to Make $10K in 10 Days With Costco Returns](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kEV86fy-Rvc), got 798,000 views, 10.9x his median.

### 3. Short owner interviews with the number in the title

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/PYnqJpvUw3g/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PYnqJpvUw3g)

- **Proof:** [UpFlip Highlights](https://www.youtube.com/@upfliphighlights) · [He Turned a Simple Idea Into a $65,000 A Day Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PYnqJpvUw3g) · 403,000 views, 22.4x, Oct 2026
- **Why it fits:** Koerner's guest videos run 30 to 70 minutes. UpFlip wins with 20-minute cuts of interviews like his.

- **Copy:** an object on the thumbnail and a per-day or per-month figure next to it.
- **Avoid:** AI tool videos without a money target. [This Tool Makes AI Agents Stupid Simple](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y33ecMVHxNU) got 31,000 views, 0.42x his median.
- **Positioning:** the owner of the business on camera, with the real numbers, where faceless rivals only narrate.
