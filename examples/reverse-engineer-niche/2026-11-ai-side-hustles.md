# 🧭 Reverse Engineering: AI side hustles and automation
Run: 2026-11-19 · Live TubeLab MCP · `skills/reverse-engineer-niche/SKILL.md`.

- **What wins:** a money promise made concrete, with a price, a buyer or a deadline. Twelve winners beat their own channel's median by 2x to 1,464x.
- **Sample:** 12 channels (12 emerging, 0 established) · 12 winners and 11 flops cited · 28 thumbnails viewed · 12 openings read
- **Start with:** the "I let AI make me money" test. It works on three channels from 3K to 51K subscribers, and anyone with a laptop and a week can film one.

Four channels with English titles but Hindi audio, and one in French, were left out.

## 📺 The Shelf

- **Format:** one person on camera, then screen recordings of the tools, the store or the payout.
- **Length:** mostly 9 to 23 minutes. Full courses of 30 minutes and more also win.
- **Where to differ:** keep the format, but put a price, a buyer or a deadline in the title. The vague versions flop.

## 🧩 Patterns That Work

### 1. The laziest way, for people with nothing · 3 winners on 3 channels

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/DWAxo1Hwvyc/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DWAxo1Hwvyc) [![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/2wwkGPNmy_I/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2wwkGPNmy_I) [![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/XrU6hYDmQrk/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XrU6hYDmQrk)

- **Title formula:** "[Laziest / Easiest] Way to Make Money with AI ([If You're Broke / If You Have No Money / In Year])"
- **Thumbnail:** [face, calm, right] + [the product as an equation, PDF + PNG = cash, or 1 hour = $100] + [one tool logo]
- **Hook:** doubt → proof of money → name the method
- **Angle:** money with almost no effort and no starting cash.
- **Anchors:** ChatGPT, Claude, Etsy, "lazy", "broke".
- **New and specific:** the method is concrete, such as $6 PNG files on Etsy, and the thumbnail shows the product.
- **Who can copy it:** anyone, but it needs real sales to show.

### 3. Sell one AI service to local businesses, at a price · 3 winners on 3 channels

[![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/BwqNVrInMrU/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BwqNVrInMrU) [![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/zVlqXgQLcag/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVlqXgQLcag) [![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/XYMcBrFSJ4c/mqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XYMcBrFSJ4c)

- **Title formula:** "I Built One [AI Thing] And Sell It To Every [Buyer] ([$ Price] Each)" or "Stop Selling [Old Service]. Sell [AI Service]"
- **Thumbnail:** [price and product, "$5000 AI EMPLOYEE"] + [the product on screen, dashboard or glowing site] + [face, small, side]
- **Hook:** the version you have seen → why it falls short → the version I run
- **Angle:** one thing you build once and sell many times to businesses that already pay.
- **Anchors:** local business, AI employee, AI receptionist, Claude Code.
- **New and specific:** a named product, a named buyer and a price per client.
- **Who can copy it:** needs a hard skill: building and selling the service.
