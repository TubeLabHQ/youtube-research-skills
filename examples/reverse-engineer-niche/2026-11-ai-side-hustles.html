Research

The niche

Videos that show how to earn a side income with AI tools: digital products, services sold to local businesses, and tests where AI is asked to make money.

Who watches
Beginners, often with a job or no money, who want their first dollar.
What wins
A money promise made concrete, with a price, a buyer or a deadline. 57 winners beat their own channel by 2× to 1,258×.
Start with
The "laziest way, if you're broke" video. It won on five channels from 3K to 659K subscribers, and anyone with a laptop can film one.

Working formats: what the winners share

Format
One person on camera, then screen recordings of the tool, the store or the payout.
Length
Mostly 8 to 23 minutes. Free courses of 1.5 to 4.5 hours also win.
Where to differ
Keep the format, but put a price, a buyer or a deadline in the title. Tool news and agency income claims flop.
