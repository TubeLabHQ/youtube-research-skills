🧭 Reverse Engineering: AI side hustles and automation

2026-11-19 · 20 channels (17 emerging, 3 established) · 57 winners and 45 flops counted, 80 videos cited

🧭 The Niche

Videos that show how to earn a side income with AI tools: digital products, services sold to local businesses, and tests where AI is asked to make money. The viewers are beginners, often with a job or no money, who want their first dollar.

  • What wins: a money promise made concrete, with a price, a buyer or a deadline. 57 winners beat their own channel's median by 2x to 1,258x.
  • Start with: the "laziest way, if you're broke" video. It won on five channels from 3K to 659K subscribers, and anyone with a laptop can film one.
  • Format: one person on camera, then screen recordings of the tool, the store or the payout.
  • Length: mostly 8 to 23 minutes. Free courses of 1.5 to 4.5 hours also win.
  • Where to differ: keep the format, but put a price, a buyer or a deadline in the title. Tool news and agency income claims flop.
