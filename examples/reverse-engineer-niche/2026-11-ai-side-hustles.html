Research

The niche

Videos that show one way to earn money with AI tools, made by a person on camera who says what it paid.

Who watches
Mostly men aged 18 to 24 with a low income, most of them in Northern America. This is an estimate, from the 4 channels with audience data.
What wins
58 winners on 21 channels. The middle winner got 72,000 views, and 44 of the 58 are also TubeLab outliers against their whole channel.
Start with
A numbered list of AI side hustles for one named group, such as students or people with a 9-to-5. It wins 12 times on 7 channels, small ones included.

Working formats: what the winners share

Format
One person explains one way to earn with AI, then shows proof: a store page, a payout screen or a test with a deadline.
Length
Long. The middle winner runs 18 minutes; half run between 12 and 29 minutes. Courses run 4 to 5 hours.
Faceless or on camera
0 of 58 winners are on faceless channels, from the hits and checked on the thumbnails. The one channel flagged faceless, Anthony Shoosh, shows his face on 2 of his 3 winning thumbnails.
Where to differ
Name who it is for (students, over 50, a 9-to-5) and give the result a number and a deadline.
