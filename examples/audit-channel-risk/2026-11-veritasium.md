## ✅ Actions To Reduce Risk
1. **Vary the caption layout of your thumbnails.**
   - **Why:** Ten of the twelve newest put a short white serif caption on one dark image, which reads as a template.
2. **Make new filming the core of any compilation.**
   - **Why:** In the robot supercut, the two chapters marked as updates run about 11 of its 87 minutes.
3. **Rewrite the About text to say who makes the videos and how.**
   - **Why:** It only says "An element of truth - videos about science, education, and anything else we find interesting."
