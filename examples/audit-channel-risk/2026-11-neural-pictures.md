## ✅ Actions To Reduce Risk
1. **Retire the "Unaware He Was a … Legend" storyline.**
   - **Why:** Three uploads in five days share its title, thumbnail and arc, and the 11 Nov video reuses the top hit's title almost word for word.
2. **Build hooks and thumbnails on the adventure, not on someone being humiliated.**
   - **Why:** Milk poured on an old man, a pulled beard and a mocked wheelchair user are the hook in all three.
3. **Make every upload a new film.**
   - **Why:** The marathon, the preview and the trailer repeat footage from the channel's own films, which adds uploads without new work.
4. **Post one on-camera video on how a film gets made, and name yourself in the About text.**
   - **Why:** No video shows the person behind the channel, and the About text says "I spent $40 and 2 hours on every minute of the film" without a name.
