Actions to reduce risk

  1. Try new title formulas
    78 of the last 120 titles share one shape, "The Most or Worst [things]", which is what makes a channel look made from a template.
  2. Cover places and stories you have not covered before
    "Most Guarded Places on Earth You Can't Visit" repeats 5 of its 9 places from "The Most Forbidden Places on Earth That You're Not Allowed to Visit".
  3. Add a real presenter on camera, in a video about the channel and how its videos are made
    No one appears on camera. The newest video shows the artist's and editor's screens, but no person.
  4. Name who makes the videos in the About text
    The About text mentions "a real human artist" but never says who runs the channel, draws or edits it.
