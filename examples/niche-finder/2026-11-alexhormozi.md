## 4. Pitfalls and what we did not check

- Estimates: revenue, RPM and audience are TubeLab model outputs shown as bands. Two channels show a revenue band with `adsense: false` (Cost Anatomy, BloodWater Deals): read those as "could earn this if monetized".
- Thin or noisy passes: both money passes returned only 40 channels for the three queries and about half were unrelated niches (Dale Builds It woodworking, TR Machine CNC, POV Ordinarylife off-grid, Peter Hartwieg gaming, Market Ghosts, HUBERMOTIVATION, Confidential: Secret History, WINTEAM REBUILD HOME, FlyingWrenchVa, Solomon's Ledger, Magnus Halvsoren, Aiyam). A money pass with this many filters is a narrow index slice, not a niche sample.
- Audience flags: the POV story channels and most economics explainers predict low-income or South Asian audiences; the Northern America upper-middle audience sits on the documentary cluster (A) and the operator channels (F). Prefer A and F parents when AdSense income is the goal.
- Likely paid placements in the outlier pass: Bookkeepers (@bookkeepersdotcom) · "Own a Successful Bookkeeping Business" · 1,894,727 · ratio 38.4 · 79 seconds · 5 comments, and Lucas Jensen (@Lucas.Jensen) · "Lessons From a $5 Million Painting Business Owner" · 281,898 · ratio 31.6 · 3 likes. Excluded from the patterns.
- Index versus live: the search index's median for @RussellRaathOfficial was 19,000 and @razvanparaschivpod 29,000; the free `get_channel` reads returned 35,000 and 9,200 on smaller live samples. Proof lines use the live read where one was made.
- Not checked: Shorts, other languages, sponsor rates, comments.
